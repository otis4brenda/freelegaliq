How Our Calculators Are Built

Every legal cost calculator is intended as a budgeting model rather than a substitute for an official worksheet, a professional quote, or individualized legal advice. Our research process is:

  • Official sources first: Fixed fees and legal requirements are checked against the responsible court, legislature, regulator, or government agency whenever such a source is available.
  • Market ranges labeled: Attorney and service costs vary. We use broad planning ranges and do not present them as official prices or guaranteed quotes.
  • Government fee schedules: For immigration matters, USCIS fee schedules are sourced directly from uscis.gov. For bankruptcy, filing fees come from the U.S. Courts fee schedule published at uscourts.gov.
  • Limitations disclosed: State selectors and regional adjustments simplify rules that may depend on county, court, income definitions, eligibility, evidence, and other facts the calculator does not collect.

Primary Data Sources

Our legal cost calculators draw on the following authoritative sources:

  • USCIS filing-fee information — Immigration form fees and fee instructions.
  • U.S. Courts bankruptcy fee schedule — Federal Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 filing charges.
  • Office of Child Support Services directory — Starting point for official state guidelines and calculators.
  • USA.gov state-court directory — State court rules, self-help material, and local fee schedules.
  • USA.gov motor-vehicle directory — State-specific license and driving-record agencies.
  • U.S. Small Business Administration — Official starting guidance for business registration.

Content Updates and Use of Drafting Tools

Software tools may assist with research organization, calculations, consistency checks, and early drafts. They do not make a calculator legally authoritative. Material should not be marked reviewed until its claims, source links, visible FAQs, structured data, and calculator behavior have been checked.

Scheduled Reviews

We periodically review the calculator inventory and prioritize pages with changed official fees, reported errors, expiring data, or high-risk jurisdictional claims.

Triggered Updates

When USCIS publishes a new fee rule, when a state enacts significant traffic fine changes, or when our sources publish updated billing rate surveys, we update affected calculators within 4 weeks. USCIS fee changes in particular are applied promptly because they directly affect calculator accuracy.

Dates Mean Substantive Review

A "last reviewed" or sitemap modification date is changed only after a substantive source, formula, explanatory, or usability review. Dates are not rotated merely to make a page appear fresh.

Accuracy & Corrections Policy

Legal costs vary significantly based on your specific jurisdiction, the complexity of your matter, the attorney you choose, and many other factors outside our control. Our calculators are designed to give you a realistic cost range for planning purposes — not a guaranteed quote.

If you believe a calculator contains an error — an incorrect filing fee, an outdated USCIS form cost, or a rate benchmark that's significantly off — please contact us at support@freelegaliq.com with the subject "Calculator Error." Include the calculator name, your state/jurisdiction, the specific figure in question, and what you believe the correct figure to be (with a source if possible).

We investigate all reported errors and apply verified corrections promptly. Because legal costs are jurisdictionally specific, we appreciate when users flag state-specific discrepancies from official court sources.
