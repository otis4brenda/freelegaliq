How we research, build, and maintain our legal cost calculators.

How Our Calculators Are Built

Every legal cost calculator on FreeLegalIQ is developed through a structured research process:

  • Court filing fee verification: Filing fees are sourced directly from state court websites, state judicial branch fee schedules, and NCSC (National Center for State Courts) data. Fees are state-specific and verified against official publications.
  • Attorney rate benchmarking: Attorney hourly rates are sourced from the National Law Journal billing rate surveys, Clio Legal Trends Report, Thomson Reuters State of U.S. Small Law Firms Report, and peer-reviewed legal economics research.
  • Government fee schedules: For immigration matters, USCIS fee schedules are sourced directly from uscis.gov. For bankruptcy, filing fees come from the U.S. Courts fee schedule published at uscourts.gov.
  • Regional cost variation: Where cost data varies significantly by geography, our calculators incorporate state-level or regional variations sourced from published government and industry data.

Primary Data Sources

Our legal cost calculators draw on the following authoritative sources:

  • USCIS Fee Schedule (uscis.gov) — Official filing fees for visa applications, green card petitions, naturalization, and other immigration forms used in our Immigration Fee Calculator.
  • U.S. Courts (uscourts.gov) — Federal bankruptcy filing fees for Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 used in our Bankruptcy Cost Calculator.
  • National Center for State Courts (NCSC) — State court filing fee data, civil case cost surveys, and judicial statistics referenced across multiple calculators.
  • Clio Legal Trends Report — Annual survey of attorney hourly rates by practice area, firm size, and geography — primary source for attorney fee benchmarks.
  • National Law Journal Billing Survey — Peer-reviewed attorney billing rate data referenced for high-complexity matter estimates.
  • State DMV & Traffic Court Websites — State-specific traffic fine schedules and license reinstatement fee data used in our Speeding Ticket and DUI Cost Calculators.
  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) — Bankruptcy process information and consumer rights guidance referenced in our Bankruptcy Cost Calculator.
  • State Small Business Portals — LLC formation filing fees and annual report requirements sourced from official state government portals for all 50 states.

Content Update Schedule

Legal costs change frequently — USCIS updates its fee schedule, states revise traffic fine schedules, and attorney billing rates shift with the market. Our editorial team maintains this update cadence:

Annual Reviews (minimum)

Every January, we conduct a full review of all calculators against published fee schedules, USCIS updates, and new court fee schedules for the new year.

Triggered Updates

When USCIS publishes a new fee rule, when a state enacts significant traffic fine changes, or when our sources publish updated billing rate surveys, we update affected calculators within 4 weeks. USCIS fee changes in particular are applied promptly because they directly affect calculator accuracy.

Annual Attorney Rate Review

We update attorney fee benchmarks annually when the Clio Legal Trends Report and National Law Journal surveys publish new data — typically each fall.

All pages display a "Last updated" date to indicate when content was most recently reviewed.

Accuracy & Corrections Policy

Legal costs vary significantly based on your specific jurisdiction, the complexity of your matter, the attorney you choose, and many other factors outside our control. Our calculators are designed to give you a realistic cost range for planning purposes — not a guaranteed quote.

If you believe a calculator contains an error — an incorrect filing fee, an outdated USCIS form cost, or a rate benchmark that's significantly off — please contact us at support@freelegaliq.com with the subject "Calculator Error." Include the calculator name, your state/jurisdiction, the specific figure in question, and what you believe the correct figure to be (with a source if possible).

We investigate all reported errors and apply verified corrections promptly. Because legal costs are jurisdictionally specific, we appreciate when users flag state-specific discrepancies from official court sources.
