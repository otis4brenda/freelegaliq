Frequently Asked Questions

Are your calculators free to use?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, no credit card, no catch. We make money from display advertising, not from referring users to law firms or selling leads.

Do you sell my personal information?

No. We don't collect personal information. All calculator inputs stay in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Are you affiliated with any law firm?

No. FreeLegalIQ is fully independent. We don't provide legal services, we're not licensed attorneys, and we have no financial relationship with any law firm or bar association. Our calculators are educational tools to help you understand approximate costs — not legal advice.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

Our calculators use 2026 data from publicly available sources including state court filing fee schedules, attorney survey data, and legal industry reports. Estimates are designed to show a realistic range — actual costs vary significantly based on location, case complexity, and the attorney you choose. Always consult a licensed attorney for guidance on your specific situation.

How often is the data updated?

We review and update our calculators at least quarterly. Court filing fees, attorney rate surveys, and state-specific data are updated annually or when significant changes occur. If you spot data that seems out of date, please let us know.

Can I use your data in an article or research project?

Yes, with attribution. Please cite FreeLegalIQ.com and the specific calculator as your source. For commercial data licensing or bulk use, contact support@freelegaliq.com.
