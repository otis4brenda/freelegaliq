Why FreeLegalIQ Exists

Legal information online is either buried in attorney websites designed to generate consultations, or so vague it's useless. People facing a divorce, a bankruptcy decision, an LLC formation, or a small claims filing often have no idea what it's going to cost — or what the process actually involves — until they're already in it.

Brenda built FreeLegalIQ to change that. Not to replace an attorney — the disclaimers are clear on that — but to give people a realistic picture of what things cost and what to expect, so they can walk into any legal situation informed rather than blindsided.

She writes and reviews all editorial content on FreeLegalIQ, drawing on her own research and experience navigating legal and business decisions across her career.
