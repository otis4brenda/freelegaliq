# Free IQ Decision Guide Standard

## Product promise

Help a visitor understand the full decision landscape, narrow the options that deserve investigation, and compare a personal shortlist without an account, contact form, or required affiliate click.

## Required experience

1. Show every material option, not an artificial two-choice contest.
2. Ask only questions that materially change which options deserve attention.
3. Return two or more paths when the facts do not support a single answer.
4. Explain why each path appeared and what fact could change the result.
5. Compare cost, time, effort, flexibility, principal risks, and professional-help triggers.
6. Link important claims to primary or authoritative sources and state the data date.
7. Run in the visitor's browser; do not transmit answers or require identity/contact information.
8. Keep every calculator, result, comparison, and explanation free.
9. Keep monetization optional and visually separate from the decision result.
10. Never imply legal, financial, or insurance advice or promise an outcome.

## Reusable formats

- **Quick narrower:** one question reveals the most relevant paths and next questions.
- **Multi-step guide:** several material questions build a shortlist.
- **Comparison builder:** visitor selects two to four options for a side-by-side table.
- **Scenario comparison:** the same inputs are calculated under multiple assumptions.

## Result anatomy

- Options worth investigating
- Why they appeared
- Advantages and tradeoffs
- Estimated cost/time ranges when supportable
- Important risks and exclusions
- Facts that could change the result
- Questions to ask a qualified professional or official agency
- Primary sources and review date

## Competitive distinction

Free IQ Decision Guides are educational decision-support tools, not quote funnels. A visitor receives the complete result without submitting contact information. Affiliate links may appear only after the result, must be disclosed, and are never required to use the tool.
