About FreeLegalIQ

We built the tool we wished existed when facing a legal situation without knowing what it would cost.

Why We Built This

Legal costs are one of the most opaque expenses in American life. When you're facing a divorce, a DUI, an estate planning decision, or a business formation, you shouldn't have to call three attorneys just to get a ballpark number. The existing resources are either vague, buried behind lead-generation forms that hand your information to law firms, or produced by the very services trying to sell you something.

FreeLegalIQ exists to change that. We built plain-language calculators that give you real cost estimates based on your state, your situation, and your choices — with no email required, no sales pitch, and no data sold to attorneys or legal services companies. You use the tool, get your answer, and walk away informed.

Our Principles

  • 🔒No personal data collected. Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers or any third party.
  • 🚫Not affiliated with any law firm or legal service. We don't receive commissions, referral fees, or payments from attorneys. Our tools give you independent estimates.
  • 📊Based on real data. Our calculators use current 2026 data from court filing records, state fee schedules, USCIS official fee charts, and industry surveys of attorney billing rates.
  • 💡Plain language only. We explain every term, every number, and every result in everyday English — no legalese, no jargon without explanation.
  • 🔄Kept current. Our content is reviewed and updated regularly to reflect current court fees, laws, and legal cost benchmarks.

How We Make Money

FreeLegalIQ displays Google AdSense advertisements. These are standard display ads — not attorney lead forms, not affiliate links to specific legal services. The ads shown are based on your browser's ad settings and are managed entirely by Google. We do not share your personal information to serve ads.

This advertising model lets us offer all calculators permanently free with no paywalls, no required email sign-ups, and no subscription fees. Ever.

Important Disclaimer

FreeLegalIQ's calculators provide estimates only. They are educational tools, not legal advice. Actual legal costs vary significantly based on your specific circumstances, the complexity of your case, the attorneys involved, and your jurisdiction.

Before making any legal decision, consult with a licensed attorney in your state. Nothing on this site creates an attorney-client relationship. For immigration matters, consult a licensed immigration attorney or accredited representative.
