About FreeLegalIQ

Independent legal cost calculators — free to use, no data selling, and no affiliation with any law firm.

Published by Brenda Nelson, Founder and Publisher  ·  Last updated: September 2026

Why We Built This

Legal costs are one of the most opaque expenses in American life. When you're facing a divorce, a DUI, an estate planning decision, or a business formation, you shouldn't have to call three attorneys just to get a ballpark number. The existing resources are either vague, buried behind lead-generation forms that hand your information to law firms, or produced by the very services trying to sell you something.

FreeLegalIQ exists to change that. We built plain-language calculators that create planning ranges based on your state, situation, and choices — with no email required and no data sold to attorneys or legal services companies. You can use the tools without requesting a quote or speaking with a salesperson.

Our Principles

  • 🔒Calculator inputs stay private. Entries are processed in your browser and are not submitted to FreeLegalIQ. Like most websites, third-party analytics and advertising services may process ordinary device and usage information as explained in our Privacy Policy.
  • ⚖️Editorially independent. We are not a law firm and no legal provider controls our calculator results. Some pages contain clearly labeled affiliate links that may earn us a commission at no additional cost to you.
  • 📊Sources and limits disclosed. We use official fee schedules where available and broad market ranges for variable professional costs. Each result remains an estimate, and our methodology page explains what the tools cannot determine.
  • 💡Plain language only. We explain every term, every number, and every result in everyday English — no legalese, no jargon without explanation.
  • 🔄Kept current. Our content is reviewed and updated regularly to reflect current court fees, laws, and legal cost benchmarks.

What Our Calculators Cover

FreeLegalIQ currently offers 10 free legal cost calculators covering the situations people search for most. Each calculator was built from a combination of court filing fee schedules, state-published data, U.S. government agency fee charts, and industry surveys of attorney billing rates.

  • ⚖️Divorce Cost Calculator — Estimates total divorce costs based on your state, whether it's contested, and how long proceedings take.
  • 🚗DUI Cost Calculator — Covers fines, attorney fees, ignition interlock, license reinstatement, and insurance increases by state.
  • 📜Estate Planning Cost Calculator — Wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and probate costs based on estate complexity.
  • 🏢LLC Formation Cost Calculator — State filing fees, registered agent costs, and annual report fees across all 50 states.
  • 👶Child Support Calculator — Estimates monthly child support obligations using income shares and percentage-of-income models.
  • 🌎Immigration Fee Calculator — USCIS filing fees, biometrics, and attorney costs for common visa and green card applications.
  • 💼Personal Injury Settlement Calculator — Estimates case value based on medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering multipliers.
  • 🏛️Small Claims Court Calculator — Filing fees and limits by state, plus what to expect from the process.
  • 🚦Speeding Ticket Cost Calculator — Fines, traffic school, and insurance impact by speed and state.
  • 💳Bankruptcy Cost Calculator — Chapter 7 vs Chapter 13 filing fees, attorney costs, and credit counseling requirements.

Important Disclaimer

FreeLegalIQ's calculators provide estimates only. They are educational tools designed to help you understand the range of costs you might face — not substitutes for professional legal advice. Actual legal costs vary significantly based on your specific circumstances, the complexity of your case, the attorneys you choose, and the jurisdiction where your matter is filed.

Before making any legal decision, consult with a licensed attorney in your state. Nothing on this site creates an attorney-client relationship, and no content on FreeLegalIQ should be construed as legal advice. For immigration matters specifically, consult a licensed immigration attorney or a Board of Immigration Appeals (BIA) accredited representative.

We update our data regularly, but court fees, attorney rates, and legal requirements change. Always verify costs with your court, your attorney, or the relevant government agency before making financial decisions.
